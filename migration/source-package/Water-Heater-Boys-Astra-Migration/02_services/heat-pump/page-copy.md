# Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installations

Hybrid heat pump water heaters are one of the most efficient water heating options available, but they require careful planning to perform correctly. Space requirements, airflow, electrical capacity, and condensate drainage all need to be addressed before installation.

Water Heater Boys installs hybrid heat pump water heaters for Bay Area homeowners looking to reduce energy use and operating costs. These systems are commonly installed as replacements for gas tank water heaters and are well suited for garages and utility spaces with adequate airflow.

Each installation includes replacement of water supply lines, installation of required drain pans, and proper condensate routing. A dedicated high voltage electrical line will need to be routed to the new unit. We evaluate electrical connections, clearances, and noise considerations to ensure the system operates as intended.

Hybrid systems are installed using manufacturer recommended configurations to preserve efficiency, reliability, and warranty coverage. If site conditions require modifications, those are addressed up front rather than discovered later.

Once installed, the system is tested and configured for proper operation before the job is complete.
