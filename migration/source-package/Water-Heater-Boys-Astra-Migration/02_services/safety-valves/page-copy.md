# Gas & Water Shut-Off Valve Installation in the San Francisco Bay Area

Protect your home from gas leaks, burst pipes, and earthquake damage with a professionally installed safety valve. Water Heater Boys provides fast, code compliant installation for earthquake gas shutoff valves and smart water shutoff systems.

## Earthquake Gas Shut-Off Valve
Earthquake gas shut-off valves are designed to automatically cut off your gas supply during seismic activity. We handle full installation including sizing, placement, and code-compliant setup.

## Moen Flo Water Shut-Off Valve
Smart water shut-off valves monitor your home’s water flow and automatically close the main line if they detect leaks, pipe bursts, or abnormal usage. Our certified installers set up the device, connect it to Wi-Fi, integrate the app on your phone, and ensure the system is calibrated correctly.
