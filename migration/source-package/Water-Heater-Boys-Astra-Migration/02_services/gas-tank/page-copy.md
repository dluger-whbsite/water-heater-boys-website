# Gas Tank Water Heater Installations

Gas tank water heater installations require more than simply replacing an old unit. Proper sizing, venting, gas supply, and safety components all need to be evaluated to ensure reliable performance and code compliant operation.

Water Heater Boys specializes in residential gas tank water heater installations throughout the Bay Area. We install standard and high efficiency gas tank units for single family homes, condos, and small multi unit properties.

Every installation includes replacement of the existing hot and cold water supply lines, replacement of the gas connector and gas shut off valve, and installation of a drain pan when one is not already present. We also evaluate venting, seismic strapping, temperature and pressure relief piping, and combustion air to confirm the installation meets current California code and manufacturer requirements.

Whenever possible, we install Bradford White gas tank water heaters. These professional grade units are not sold through big box retailers and are known for long term reliability and durable internal components. If a different manufacturer is required due to availability or customer preference, we install only reputable professional grade equipment.

Our installations include removal and disposal of the existing water heater, installation of the new unit, and verification of safe operation. Each system is tested for gas leaks and proper draft before the job is complete.
