# Gas Tankless Water Heater Installations

Gas tankless water heater installations involve more planning and technical evaluation than traditional tank systems. Gas sizing, venting configuration, water quality, and overall demand all need to be considered to ensure consistent hot water and long term reliability.

Water Heater Boys installs gas tankless water heaters for homes across the Bay Area, including both first time conversions and replacements of existing tankless units. Each project starts with an evaluation of the gas line capacity, vent routing, condensate drainage, and available installation space.

Every installation includes replacement of water supply connections, installation of new gas shut off components, and proper condensate management. When required, we address gas line upgrades and venting changes as part of the installation to meet manufacturer specifications and local code requirements.

We install professional grade tankless water heaters designed for long term residential use, not retail grade units intended for limited applications. Our goal is a clean, serviceable installation that delivers stable performance without shortcuts.

Before completion, each system is tested to confirm proper ignition, temperature stability, and safe operation.
