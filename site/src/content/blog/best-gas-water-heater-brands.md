# Best Gas Water Heater Brands: Bradford White vs. A. O. Smith, State & Rheem

**Which gas water heater brand is actually the best?**

If you're replacing a gas water heater, you'll probably come across familiar names like Bradford White, A. O. Smith, State, and Rheem. They all make residential water heaters, but there are differences in their product lines, components, warranties, and how they're sold and serviced.

At Water Heater Boys, we install and service water heaters throughout the San Francisco Bay Area. We have our preferences based on the equipment we work with, the features we value, and the support available when something goes wrong.

Here's how we rank four of the best-known gas water heater brands and what homeowners should understand before choosing one.

## 1. Bradford White: Our Top Choice

When it comes to traditional gas tank water heaters, **Bradford White is our first choice** for most residential installations.

Bradford White is an American manufacturer that distributes its water heaters through professional plumbing supply channels rather than big-box retail stores. Its products are designed to be selected, installed, and serviced by plumbing professionals.

One reason we favor Bradford White is its attention to tank protection and components.

Its residential gas water heaters include several notable features:

- **Vitraglas® Tank Lining:** A protective coating designed to help resist corrosion inside the steel tank.
- **Hydrojet® Total Performance System:** Helps reduce sediment accumulation, which can affect water heater efficiency and performance.
- **Bradford White ICON System®:** An intelligent gas control system that helps regulate water temperature.
- **Defender Safety System®:** Provides protection against ignition of flammable vapors outside the water heater.

We also appreciate the professional supply network. When a water heater needs replacement parts or warranty assistance, having an established relationship with a plumbing distributor can make the process easier.

### Bradford White Warranty

Most standard Bradford White residential gas water heaters carry a **six-year limited warranty on the tank and component parts**.

For eligible models, Bradford White offers its BUILTBEST® Extended Warranty program. At Water Heater Boys, we offer the BUILTBEST +4 upgrade, extending the original six-year coverage to ten years.

This is an important option for homeowners who want additional protection against unexpected equipment failures.

**Our verdict:** Bradford White earns our top recommendation because of its construction features, professional distribution model, and the warranty support available through our suppliers.

## 2. A. O. Smith: A Strong Alternative

A. O. Smith is one of the largest and most established names in water heating. It manufactures residential and commercial water heaters and offers multiple product lines designed for different markets.

One distinction homeowners should understand is that A. O. Smith sells both professionally distributed and retail-oriented products.

Its **ProLine® series** is the lineup we're focusing on in this comparison. The company also sells Signature models through Lowe's, and those products may have different features and specifications.

A. O. Smith's ProLine gas water heaters offer several noteworthy features:

- **Blue Diamond® Glass Lining:** Designed to protect the interior tank from corrosion.
- **CoreGard™ Anode Rod:** Helps protect the steel tank by attracting corrosive elements in the water.
- **DynaClean™ Diffuser Dip Tube:** Helps reduce sediment buildup inside the tank.
- **Brass Drain Valve:** Found on the standard ProLine gas lineup, providing a durable component for draining and servicing the tank.

### A. O. Smith Warranty

Standard ProLine gas water heaters typically come with a six-year limited tank and parts warranty, while certain ProLine Master models offer eight years.

A. O. Smith also provides extended warranty kits for eligible models. Some of these can increase a six-year warranty to ten years.

What makes certain A. O. Smith extended warranty kits interesting is that they include additional anode protection installed by the plumber. The anode helps protect the interior of the tank against corrosion.

Water Heater Boys offers qualifying A. O. Smith warranty upgrades as part of our installation options.

**Our verdict:** A. O. Smith is a solid alternative to Bradford White, particularly its professionally distributed ProLine models. We rank it second because Bradford White remains our preferred equipment and supply network for standard residential replacements.

## 3. State Water Heaters: A Reliable, Less Familiar Name

State is another established water heater brand that homeowners may encounter when comparing gas water heaters.

Something many people don't realize is that **State is owned by A. O. Smith**. The company acquired State Industries in 2001.

That relationship explains why the brands share some technology and product features.

State's residential gas lineup includes the ProLine, ProLine Master, and ProLine XE families, with features such as Blue Diamond glass lining, DynaClean sediment reduction, and CoreGard anode protection.

These are credible features, and State should not be overlooked simply because its name is less familiar to homeowners.

### State Water Heater Warranty

Standard State ProLine gas water heaters generally carry six-year limited tank and parts warranties. ProLine Master models commonly carry eight-year warranties, with additional options depending on the model.

**Our verdict:** State makes a competitive residential gas water heater. Since it shares corporate ownership and some technology with A. O. Smith, the actual model specifications and local service support are more useful comparisons than the name on the tank. We place it third in our preferred lineup.

## 4. Rheem: Popular at Home Depot, but Not Our First Choice

Rheem is one of the most recognizable water heater brands in the country, partly because its products are widely sold through Home Depot.

Rheem manufactures a broad range of water heaters, including its retail-oriented Performance lineup and its professionally distributed Professional series.

That's an important distinction. Not every Rheem water heater has the same components, features, or warranty coverage.

Rheem's retail models can be attractive to homeowners because they're readily available, have clearly advertised pricing, and offer several warranty choices.

However, **Rheem is not our preferred brand for a professionally supplied gas water heater installation**.

Our preference is to use equipment obtained through our established plumbing distributors, where we're familiar with the specific models, parts availability, and warranty procedures.

We also encourage homeowners to compare the actual components of a water heater rather than relying entirely on the brand name. Features such as drain-valve construction, anode protection, and gas controls can vary between product lines.

### Rheem Warranty

Rheem offers several warranty levels on its residential gas water heaters.

Many standard Performance models include six-year limited tank and parts coverage, along with one year of in-home labor coverage. Certain Performance Plus models offer nine-year tank and parts warranties, while selected Performance Platinum models offer twelve-year coverage.

Some higher-tier models also include longer labor warranties.

That's worth recognizing. Rheem offers competitive warranty options, even though it isn't our preferred brand.

**Our verdict:** Rheem makes legitimate water heaters with several product and warranty options. We rank it fourth based on our preference for other professional product lines, not because every Rheem model is poorly made.

## Which Gas Water Heater Brand Has the Best Warranty?

This is one of the most common questions homeowners ask when shopping for a replacement water heater.

Most standard residential gas water heaters start with a six-year limited manufacturer's warranty, but the differences become more interesting when you consider extended coverage.

| Brand | Standard coverage | Longer coverage options |
|---|---|---|
| Bradford White | 6 years | 10 years with BUILTBEST +4 |
| A. O. Smith ProLine | 6 years | 10 years with eligible extension kits |
| State ProLine | 6 years | Longer coverage on qualifying models |
| Rheem Performance | 6 years | Selected 9- and 12-year models |

*These are representative residential gas models. Warranty terms and eligibility vary by exact model.*

### What Does a Water Heater Warranty Actually Cover?

A manufacturer's tank warranty generally protects against qualifying failures caused by manufacturing defects, including certain tank leaks.

A parts warranty covers qualifying defects in components, subject to the manufacturer's terms.

But here's something homeowners often misunderstand:

**A ten-year manufacturer's warranty doesn't necessarily mean ten years of free service calls or installation labor.**

For example, a manufacturer may provide a replacement tank under warranty while the homeowner remains responsible for labor, permits, and other costs associated with replacing it.

Some manufacturers include limited labor coverage, while others leave labor costs to the homeowner.

That's why it's important to compare what the warranty actually covers, not just how many years appear on the label.

At Water Heater Boys, our installations also come with a separate one-year workmanship warranty. We explain the applicable manufacturer coverage and available extensions before installation.

### Is an Extended Water Heater Warranty Worth It?

For many homeowners, it's worth considering.

A water heater is a significant household investment, and an extended warranty can provide protection against certain unexpected equipment failures after the standard coverage expires.

On eligible Bradford White models, our BUILTBEST +4 option extends the manufacturer's tank and parts coverage from six to ten years.

For qualifying A. O. Smith water heaters, we also offer extended warranty options that may include additional anode protection.

Keep in mind that a longer warranty isn't a guarantee that a water heater will last longer. Bradford White even notes that certain six-year and ten-year versions of its products are identical in construction.

The extension primarily gives homeowners additional financial protection.

## Other Gas Water Heater Brands Worth Knowing

There are several other names homeowners may encounter when shopping for a traditional gas water heater.

**American Standard Water Heaters** offers residential and commercial water heating equipment, including conventional gas storage models.

**American Water Heaters** and **Reliance** are part of the broader A. O. Smith brand family.

**Ruud** and **Richmond** are associated with Rheem, offering additional residential water heater options.

The important thing to understand is that there are fewer independent manufacturers than there are brand names.

When comparing these products, look at the actual model specifications, warranty coverage, and availability of service rather than assuming every brand represents a completely different manufacturing company.
