# Two Safety Upgrades Bay Area Homeowners Should Know About

There are two plumbing and gas safety upgrades we are seeing more often in Bay Area homes:

**Seismic gas shutoff valves** and **automatic water shutoff valves**.

They solve very different problems, but both are designed to stop a small emergency from becoming a much larger one.

## Seismic gas shutoff valves

A seismic gas shutoff valve is installed on the home's gas line and is designed to automatically stop the flow of gas when it detects significant earthquake movement.

That matters in the Bay Area because damaged gas piping after an earthquake can create a serious fire risk.

California certifies earthquake-actuated gas shutoff valves for residential use, while the specific installation and permit requirements are generally handled by local building departments.

At Water Heater Boys, we typically install a certified Little Firefighter seismic valve and provide documentation of the installation.

These valves are also something homeowners sometimes hear about from their insurance company, particularly when renewing or shopping for coverage.

## Automatic water shutoff valves

A device such as the **Moen Flo** is installed on the home's main water line.

It continuously monitors water use and looks for abnormal activity that may indicate a leak. If it detects a serious problem, it can automatically shut off the home's water supply and send an alert to your phone.

That can be especially useful for leaks that happen when nobody is home.

A burst supply line, failed toilet connection, or other plumbing leak can release a lot of water before someone notices it. An automatic shutoff gives the house a way to respond even if you are at work or away on vacation.

## Insurance companies are paying more attention to water shutoff systems

We increasingly hear from homeowners whose insurance company has asked them to install an automatic water shutoff device or has recommended one as part of maintaining coverage.

That does not mean every California homeowner is required to install one.

Insurance requirements vary by carrier, policy, property, and sometimes the home's claims history.

If your insurer has specifically requested a shutoff valve, send us the documentation they provided. We can help determine what device is appropriate and provide installation documentation afterward.

## Which one should you install?

They protect against different risks.

A seismic gas valve helps stop natural gas after significant earthquake movement.

An automatic water shutoff valve helps detect plumbing leaks and can stop the water before the damage becomes much worse.

Some homeowners install one because their insurance company requested it. Others install them simply because they want additional protection.

For a Moen Flo or similar water shutoff, send us photos showing where the main water line enters the house and the accessible piping around it.
