How soon can you replace my water heater?

Need hot water urgently? Call us about same-day installation. Replacements are typically scheduled within one to two days, subject to scheduling and equipment availability. We’ll confirm the earliest available appointment for your home.

Do I need a 40-, 50-, or 75-gallon tank?

We’ll help you choose based on your household’s hot water use and the existing installation. If you don’t know your current tank size, our team can help.

How much will replacement cost?

The unit, access, venting, connections, and any necessary updates affect the price. We assess your setup and provide a quote before you approve the work.

What warranty comes with the installation?

We provide a one-year labor warranty. The water heater also carries the manufacturer’s warranty, with coverage confirmed for the model selected.

Can I request a quote before my tank fails?

Yes. Let us know you’re planning ahead, and we’ll help you arrange a gas tank replacement.
